Evaporation

Imagine the sun warming up the water in the ocean.

When water gets hot, it turns into an invisible gas called water vapor. It floats up into the sky just like steam from a hot cup of tea.

Step 2 of 3

Condensation

As the water vapor goes higher, the air becomes cooler.

The cool air changes it back into tiny liquid water drops. These drops join together to form clouds .

Step 3 of 3

Precipitation

When the clouds become too heavy, they can no longer hold the water.

The water falls back to Earth as rain (or snow when it is very cold).
